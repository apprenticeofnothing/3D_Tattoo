# Capture guide: photographing my body for the scan

Goal: 150 to 250 sharp, overlapping photos of me standing still, taken
by a helper walking around me with my iPhone 16. Two to three minutes.

## Before

- Room: even, soft light. Daylight through curtains or several lamps.
  No direct sun, no flash, no strong shadows. Stand at least 1 m from
  walls and furniture, on a plain floor.
- Clothing: tight, matte, not black, ideally with a fine pattern
  (dark trousers have made legs vanish in Apple's pipeline). Skin that
  will be tattooed should be bare. Tie hair back; if it is shiny, a bit
  of dry shampoo helps. No glasses, no jewellery, no watch.
- Camera app settings: highest resolution photo mode, HDR off, Live
  Photos off, no zoom (1x main camera only). Lock focus and exposure by
  long-pressing on my torso until AE/AF LOCK appears, and do not touch
  the exposure for the whole session.
- Pose: A-pose. Feet shoulder width apart, arms about 30 degrees out
  from the body, palms facing the thighs, fingers relaxed and together,
  face forward, eyes on one fixed point. Breathe shallowly, do not talk,
  do not shift weight. Micro-movement is the number one cause of failure.

## During

The helper walks slowly around me in three closed rings, taking a photo
about every 5 to 7 degrees (roughly 50 to 70 photos per ring). Each photo
should overlap the previous one by at least 70 percent.

1. Ring 1, camera at knee height, tilted slightly up, 1 to 1.5 m away.
2. Ring 2, camera at chest height, level.
3. Ring 3, camera above head height, tilted down.

Then 15 to 25 detail photos closer in (about 50 cm) of the areas I am
considering for tattoos, each from several angles, still overlapping.

Rules for the helper: keep the whole body in frame on rings 1 to 3,
move after each shot, not during; keep the same distance; pause if a
photo is blurry and retake it; never change zoom or exposure.

## After

- Check a handful of photos at full zoom for blur. Delete the blurred
  ones.
- Transfer all photos to the Mac with AirDrop or a cable. Keep them in a
  folder outside this repo (the repo ignores photos anyway).
- Measure my height barefoot against a wall. The mesh is scaled to it.

## If standing still for three minutes is not possible

Record a slow 4K video walkaround instead (stabilisation off, same
rings), about one minute. Frames are extracted on the Mac. Expect lower
quality than stills.
