Body scan capture

For the helper holding the phone. The person being scanned stands still in an A-pose for the whole session.

  1. Even soft light, no flash, plain background, 1 m from walls.
  2. Tight, matte, patterned, not black clothing. Skin to be tattooed bare. Hair tied back.
  3. Three rings around the body: knee height, chest height, above the head. Then close-ups of the tattoo areas.
  4. Walk slowly, keep the whole body in frame, stop briefly for each shot.

Photos stay on this phone. At the end you export them as ZIP files to AirDrop to the Mac. The phone's own Camera app gives higher resolution stills; this page gives even spacing and a sharpness check. Both work.
